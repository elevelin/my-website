Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia

Published on September 24, 2026 by Amber Rasmussen

Art. Technology. Barbering. Curiosity. A little bit of everything.

Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia — a creative space for the things I make, the things I learn, and the things I can't stop being curious about.

I'm Amber. I'm a barber-in-training based in Fort Myers, Florida, but barbering is only one piece of the picture. I've always had a habit of jumping between creative worlds, sometimes with a paintbrush, sometimes with a computer, and sometimes with a pair of clippers.

I like making things. I like figuring out how things work. And more importantly, I like taking an idea that exists in my head and turning it into something real.

More Than a Barbering Page

Barbering is one of my newest creative outlets, and I'm currently building my skills and developing my own style. I'm drawn to cuts with personality, sharp details, texture, clean fades, and looks that don't feel like they came straight off an assembly line.

But you'll find plenty here that has nothing to do with hair.

Art has always been another way for me to experiment with ideas, aesthetics, and different ways of seeing things. Sometimes that means creating something intentional and polished. Other times, it's simply following an idea and seeing where it goes.

Then there's technology.

I've spent years working with computers, troubleshooting problems, building systems, automating repetitive tasks, and finding ways to make technology actually do something useful. I'm fascinated by the intersection of creativity and technology — especially when a weird idea turns into a working project.

Why "Ambrosia"?

Ambrosia was said to be the food of the gods — something rare, desirable, and almost otherworldly.

I liked the name because it felt bigger than a single profession or hobby. Touch of Ambrosia isn't meant to fit neatly into one category.

It's a place for experimentation.

One day that might mean a haircut. The next, a piece of art, a new website, a tech project, a strange idea I decided to build, or something completely unexpected.

What You'll Find Here

This site will evolve as I do. I'll be sharing barbering, hairstyles, creative work, art, technology, projects, inspiration, experiments, and some of the things I'm learning along the way.

There probably won't be a perfectly organized theme tying everything together — and that's intentional.

The common thread is simply making things.

Whether I'm creating something with my hands, designing something on a screen, writing something from scratch, or figuring out how to make an idea work, that's the kind of stuff I want to document here.

So if you're interested in barbering, art, technology, creativity, or just watching someone chase a bunch of interesting ideas...

You're in the right place.

Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia.

Make something interesting.
Break something. Figure it out.
Do it differently.

— Amber
