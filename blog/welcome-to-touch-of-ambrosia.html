Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia

Published on September 23, 2026 by Amber Rasmussen

Beauty with an edge. Style with a little bite.

Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia — a space where classic barbering meets creativity, individuality, and a little bit of rebellion.

I’m Amber, a barber-in-training based in Fort Myers, Florida, currently turning my passion for hair, style, and creative expression into a craft. I’ve always believed that the way you look can be more than just a haircut or hairstyle — it can be an attitude, a mood, or a way of showing the world exactly who you are.

That’s what Touch of Ambrosia is about.

I’m not interested in creating the same look for everyone. I love styles that have personality — clean fades, sharp details, bold transformations, textured looks, and anything that gives someone that extra something when they walk out the door.

So, What Is Ambrosia?

Ambrosia was said to be the food of the gods — something rare, desirable, and unforgettable.

I liked the idea of bringing that feeling into barbering.

A great haircut shouldn’t just look good. It should feel good.

This site will be where I share pieces of the journey: barbering, hairstyles, techniques, beauty, inspiration, transformations, and the occasional rabbit hole into whatever catches my creative attention.

I’m still learning, experimenting, and sharpening my skills — literally and figuratively. Every cut is another opportunity to get better, try something different, and create something that makes someone feel like their best version of themselves.

So whether you're here for haircut inspiration, to follow my journey into the barbering world, or just to see what I’m creating next...

Welcome to Touch of Ambrosia.

Come for the cut.
Stay for the attitude.

— Amber
